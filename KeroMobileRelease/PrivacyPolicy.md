# Kero for iOS Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Kero for iOS is a standalone SSH client. The app does not require an account
and the developer does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising data,
tracking data, saved hosts, credentials, terminal activity, or usage data.

## Information stored on your device

Kero stores saved host details, trusted server fingerprints, and app settings
in protected app storage. Passwords and private SSH keys are stored in the
iOS device-only Keychain. A credential can optionally require Face ID or the
device passcode before use.

These records are used only to provide the connections and preferences you
request. Kero does not synchronize them to a Kero service.

## Network connections

When you connect, Kero communicates directly with the SSH server you chose.
Kero does not proxy terminal traffic through a Kero service. The selected
server and its operator can receive your username, authentication attempt,
commands, and terminal traffic according to that server's own policies.

Connecting to an address on your local network may cause iOS to request local
network permission. Kero uses that permission only to reach SSH servers you
choose.

## Clipboard

A remote terminal may ask Kero to copy text to the system clipboard. Kero does
not permit a remote process to read the local clipboard.

## Retention and deletion

Information remains on the device until you delete it or remove the app. You
can delete individual hosts and keys, forget trusted server identities, or use
**Settings > Erase All Data** to remove all Kero hosts, credentials, keys,
trusted identities, and preferences from the device.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy
answers must be updated before the changed build is released.
